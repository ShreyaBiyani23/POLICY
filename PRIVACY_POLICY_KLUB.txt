PRIVACY POLICY - SHORT VERSION (For App Store Display)

KOLORS KLUB App Privacy Policy - Summary

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

---

1. WHAT DATA DO WE COLLECT?

The app collects the following information that you provide:
• Customer name, address, and phone number
• Your notes and remarks

This data is stored locally on your device only.
All other data is retrived from our own registered customers data from our server.

---

2. HOW DO WE USE YOUR DATA?

Your data is used only to:
• Create new quotation.
• Generate PDF reports with your specifications.
• Allow you to share PDFs through your chosen method.

---

3. WHO DO WE SHARE YOUR DATA WITH?

We do NOT share your data with anyone. Your data only leaves your device if:
• YOU choose to share a PDF document (you select the recipient)
• YOU choose to navigate to external websites

---

4. WHAT THIRD-PARTY LIBRARIES DO WE USE?

Library Name | Purpose | Data Sent Off-Device?
PDF Library | Create documents | NO
File Management | Store files locally | NO
Share Function | Enable sharing | ONLY when you choose to share
PDF Viewer | Display documents | NO
Localization | Language support | NO
Animations | UI effects | NO
Notifications | Show alerts | NO

---

5. DO WE USE ANALYTICS OR TRACKING?

NO. We do not use:
• Google Analytics
• Firebase Analytics
• Crash reporting (unless explicitly opted in)
• User behavior tracking
• Advertising networks

---

6. IS MY DATA BACKED UP?

• iOS: Data may be included in iCloud backups (controlled by your device settings)
• Android: Data may be included in Google backups (controlled by your device settings)
• You can disable app-specific backups in your device settings

---

7. CAN I DELETE MY DATA?

YES. You can:
• Delete individual customer profiles
• Delete all data by uninstalling the app
• Manage shared PDFs yourself

---

8. WHAT ABOUT CHILDREN?

This app is not intended for users under 13 years old.

---

9. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You have the right to:
• Access all your data (view in app)
• Correct your data (edit in app)
• Delete your data (delete in app)
• Export your data (generate PDF)

---

10. CONTACT US

Questions about privacy?
Email: servicekult@kolorsworld.com

SUMMARY: Your data stays on your device. We don't track you, share data, or use analytics. You control everything.
